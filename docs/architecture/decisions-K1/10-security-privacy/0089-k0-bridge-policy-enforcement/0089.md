# ADR-0089: K0 Bridge Policy Enforcement and Obligations

**Status**: Accepted – Implementation Pending

**Date**: 2025-10-30

**Authors**: @cascade (on behalf of FamilyOS Kernel Team)

## Context

- **Bridge exclusivity** (ADR-0001, ADR-0001a, ADR-0001f) mandates that every K1→K0 interaction traverses the K0 bridge ports. Policy admission therefore has to terminate at the bridge, yet current enforcement resides inside K0 without a formal contract.
- **Minimal Gate** validation covers signatures, schema activation, and provisioning, but policy obligations (allow/deny/redact) are not observable: redaction helpers are unimplemented, metrics are absent, and tests cannot inject policy variants.
- **CRDT mesh roadmap** (ADR-0050 family) requires that policy decisions propagate consistently across devices when K0 nodes sync via P07; missing audit/receipt annotations introduce integrity gaps for edge-only replicas.
- **Observability and compliance pressures** demand structured telemetry and audit trails for policy outcomes. Research in policy-as-code (OPA/Rego) and local-first security (Keyhive) emphasises decoupled decision points with verifiable obligations.

## Decision

- **Define a bridge-level policy contract (P00-P03)** covering: request envelope schema, decision payload (allow/deny/obligations), mandated metrics, and audit artifacts. The contract will be versioned under `k0/contracts/policy/bridge_policy.yml` and consumed by bridge clients before they invoke K0 ports.
- **Implement a K0 Policy Enforcement Module (PEM)** that materialises bridge decisions:
  - Redaction pipeline: complete `k0/policy/redaction.py` to apply field-level masking per obligation; persist sanitized payloads in WAL; emit redaction ledger entries (`redaction_log`).
  - Receipt augmentation: include decision + obligations in receipts for traceability.
  - Observability: emit counters/histograms via `MetricsExporter` (`k0_pep_decisions_total{decision}` and `k0_pep_obligations_total{type}`) and structured audit events (reuse `record_admission_decision`).
  - Failure semantics: if the bridge cannot retrieve a decision or PEM fails to apply obligations, return `503 POLICY_UNAVAILABLE` with retry advice; bridge retries within configured backoff.
- **Expose deterministic policy fixtures for tests**: allow bootstrap harness/pytest to load alternative policy manifests through kernel settings overrides, enabling ALLOW/DENY/REDACT scenarios without modifying production manifests.
- **Align with mesh sync**: ensure redaction ledger rows and obligation metadata are CRDT-friendly (monotonic, last-write-wins) so P07 replication preserves compliance state across devices.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Formalises bridge policy responsibilities, enabling contract-first development per 5-Gate workflow.
- Unlocks testability of policy behavior (allow/deny/redact) with deterministic fixtures.
- Strengthens compliance/audit posture (redaction ledger, metrics) while staying edge-only.
- Keeps P07 CRDT replication consistent by persisting obligation outcomes alongside WAL entries.

### Negative

- Increases complexity in K0 command port (extra steps before commit, new failure modes).
- Requires additional storage tables and migration (redaction log, decision metadata).
- Bridge clients must integrate the new policy contract, potentially impacting existing tooling.

### Risks

- Misconfigured redaction directives could corrupt payloads; mitigated by contract validation and end-to-end tests.
- Metrics/audit emission overhead might affect latency; mitigated with perf budgets (maintain <50 µs overhead per command).
- Contract drift between bridge and kernel versions; mitigated via semantic versioning and CI compatibility checks.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Keep policy enforcement entirely inside K0 (status quo)

- Rejected because obligations remain invisible to bridge/K1, violating ADR-0001f contract-first principles and blocking deterministic tests.

### Alternative 2: Move policy engine into K1 before bridge

- Rejected because ADR-0001 requires K0 to remain the enforcement authority for storage compliance, and edge-only guarantees demand that decisions execute on the authority storage node.

## Implementation Notes

- **Phase 1**: Author bridge policy contract, implement PEM scaffolding, complete redaction helper, add metrics/audit stubs.
- **Phase 2**: Migrate bootstrap harness tests to exercise ALLOW/DENY/REDACT using manifest overrides; add migration scripts for new tables.
- **Phase 3**: Integrate with P07 replication (ensure redaction ledger is synced) and update observability dashboards.
- **Testing**: Expand pytest/Ward suites for policy matrix; introduce performance benchmarks ensuring PEM stays within latency budgets.
- **Rollback**: Revert to previous manifest version; disable PEM via feature flag while maintaining Minimal Gate protections.
