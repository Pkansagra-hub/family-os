# K004.2: Multi-Modal Affect

**Status**: PROPOSED | **Parent**: [K004](k004-affect-service.md)

## Future Capabilities

- Image affect (facial expressions, scene composition)
- Audio affect (voice tone, speech prosody)
- Video affect (temporal emotional arcs)
- Biometric affect (heart rate, skin conductance if available)
- Cross-modal fusion (combine text + image + audio)

## Research Foundation

- Russell's Circumplex Model (valence × arousal)
- Ekman's Basic Emotions
- Multi-modal emotion recognition (late fusion)
